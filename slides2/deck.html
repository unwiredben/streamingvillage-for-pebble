Beyond the ask

Four harnesses nobody asked for

  • 01Compile the layout macros and print what they produce Real preprocessor, real integer division — no arithmetic by hand.
  • 02Composite the scene on the host, without a watch Decoding the PNGs by hand, because no image library was available.
  • 03Checksum the artwork Regenerating must leave the reference images byte-identical.
  • 04Run the watchface itself on the host Timers and graphics stubbed, every scroll position checked.

Beyond the ask

A GIF cannot hold a frame rate

It holds a delay per frame, in whole hundredths of a second. Rounding once would have played the loop 8% fast — so the delays are written into the finished file, two bytes at a time.

Beyond the ask

21% of the pixels were wrong

1 − π/4

The fraction of a square outside its circle. The round watches were reporting the colour hidden underneath their transparent corners — nothing visible was affected at all.
